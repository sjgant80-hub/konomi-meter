konomi-meter

What did your AI actually cost — and what needed to leave the machine?

Local inference gets priced at zero in every write-up, every dashboard, every "we moved it on-prem and saved 100%". The moment one side of a comparison is zero, the comparison stops being a decision. This prices both sides, attributes the spend by task type, and refuses to call anything a saving without evidence the local tier could have done the work.

Local is not free

250W for forty seconds at 27p/kWh is about 0.075p. Small, and not zero — and that difference is what lets you say "50× cheaper" instead of the useless "one costs money and one doesn't".

Attribution by task type

"You spent £41 on the frontier" prompts no decision. "Four bulk-classification jobs cost £38 of it, and that job type runs locally in nine seconds" prompts exactly one.

Cheaper is not the question

Every remote call would have been cheaper locally. The question is whether local could have done it — and without evidence the answer is UNPROVEN, kept out of the savings figure.

The three verdicts on escaped spend

VerdictMeaning
AVOIDABLEThe local tier has a recorded success rate on this task type at or above the bar. This call did not need to leave the machine — and this is the only figure that may be called a saving.
NECESSARYLocal was tried on this task type and fell below the bar. Escaping was reasonable.
UNPROVENLocal was never tried, or not enough times for the rate to mean anything. No claim either way, and reported separately rather than folded into a saving.

The fastest way to shrink your UNPROVEN column is to run the local tier on those task types once and find out. The report names them in priority order.

What the cost model excludes

A cost model's exclusions are the model, so they travel with every figure: the hardware purchase (unless you supply an amortisation rate — most people already own the machine, and the marginal cost of one more run really is just the power), your time waiting for a slow local run, and idle draw, cooling and the rest of the building. This is a marginal energy cost and it is named as one.

Sovereign share, measured twice

A thousand cheap local calls and one expensive frontier call is 99.9% local by call count and can be under 5% by money. Both are reported, because the first is the one people quote and the second is the one on the bill.

Honest limits

  • A remote run recorded without token counts is refused, not priced at zero — that is how spend disappears from a report.
  • An unrecognised tier throws rather than defaulting to free.
  • Success is a boolean you supply. If your definition of "the local model handled it" is generous, so is every figure downstream.
  • This measures what you recorded. Work that never got logged is invisible, and nothing here can tell you it is missing.
